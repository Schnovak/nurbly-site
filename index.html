Privacy policy

Last updated 18 August 2026

Nürbly does not collect anything. It has no internet permission, so there is no way for it to send data anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app stores

Nürbly keeps four things, and it keeps them on your phone:

  • your total XP
  • your best score
  • your longest streak
  • how many rounds you have played

That is the whole list. It is written to the app's own private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Android's own backup

Nürbly allows Android's standard app backup, which is the feature that restores your progress when you set up a new phone. If you have backup switched on in your Google account settings, Android may copy that score file to your Google Drive backup. Android does this, not Nürbly, and the developer never sees it or has any access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings under backup.

Permissions

Nürbly requests one permission: vibration, used for the short buzz when you answer. It does not request internet, location, storage, camera, contacts, or anything else.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no crash reporters, no social sign-in. The app ships with Google's Jetpack libraries compiled into it, and none of them phone home.

Children

Nürbly is not directed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone, so it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version replaces this page.
